# SQA Qualification Reports

Versioned Markdown reports preserve promotion decisions and their supporting evidence.
Keep current candidate reports in this directory.

Move superseded reports into `archive/<year-month>/` without rewriting their results.
Raw WAV, JSON, screenshot, trace, and browser-output trees remain outside Git.

A promotion report must record:

- Exact source, image, and chart identities.
- Pass and failure counts for every blocking suite.
- Known evidence gaps and rejected-candidate reasons.
- Links or session identifiers needed to locate retained external evidence.
